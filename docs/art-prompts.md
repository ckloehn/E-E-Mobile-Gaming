# Art Prompts for an AI Image Generator

The characters and clothes are drawn by the game's code. That's what lets every piece of clothing go
on every person, and lets arms and legs move. The **backgrounds** are where painted art makes the
biggest difference, so these prompts are for backgrounds.

Paste each prompt into an image generator such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Midjourney or Adobe Firefly.

> Don't name brands like "Barbie" in the prompt. Most generators refuse brand names, and the game
> should have its own look anyway. The wording below describes the style without naming it.

## How to add a picture to the game

1. Generate the image, then pick the one she likes best (let her choose!).
2. Save it with the **exact file name** listed below, as a `.jpg`.
3. Either drop it in the chat with Claude and say which one it is, or upload it to the `art/` folder of
   this repository on GitHub (**Add file → Upload files**, then type `art/` before the name).

The game uses the picture automatically when it exists. Until then it shows the drawn background.

## 1. Castle great hall: `art/castle-hall.jpg`

The main screen where the family walks around and chats. **Most important.**

**Size:** landscape, 3:2, at least 2400 × 1600 pixels.

```
Interior of a dreamy pastel princess castle great hall, background art for a children's fashion doll
dress-up game. Glossy, polished 2D digital illustration in the style of a modern animated fashion-doll
TV series: smooth gradients, soft glow, clean shapes, sparkles. Pink, lavender and light gold palette with
little rainbow accents. Back wall has tall arched windows showing a blue sky with a rainbow and fluffy
clouds, hanging pink and purple banners with a star and a heart, a crystal chandelier, and a heart-shaped
pink throne in the center back. Shiny pink and gold checkered marble floor fills the bottom third of the
image, with a pink carpet runner down the middle. Straight-on front view at eye level, symmetrical.
The middle and foreground floor is wide open and empty so characters can stand and walk there.
No people, no characters, no animals, no text, no logos, no watermark.
```

## 2. Fashion show runway: `art/runway-stage.jpg`

Where the doll walks and the judges give stars.

**Size:** landscape, 3:2, at least 2400 × 1600 pixels.

```
A sparkly fashion show stage for a children's dress-up game, background art. Glossy polished 2D
digital illustration in the style of a modern animated fashion-doll TV series. Straight-on front view
of a glamorous runway stage: pink velvet curtains pulled to both sides, golden spotlights shining down
from the top, a glittering purple and pink back wall with stars and little hearts, a shiny pink runway
floor in the bottom third that leads toward the viewer, soft bokeh sparkles in the air. Bright, happy,
magical. The center of the stage is empty for a character to stand. No people, no audience faces,
no characters, no text, no logos, no watermark.
```

## 3. Title screen: `art/title-background.jpg`

Behind the "Toca Toca Egg House" logo and Play button.

**Size:** square, at least 2048 × 2048 pixels (it gets trimmed to fit both iPad and phone).

```
A magical rainbow castle on a fluffy cloud at sunrise, title screen background for a children's
dress-up game called Egg House. Glossy polished 2D digital illustration, pastel pink, lavender, sky blue
and gold, sparkles and soft glow. The castle has rounded egg-shaped towers with gold crowns on top and
heart-shaped windows. A big soft rainbow arcs behind it. Keep the center of the image calm and slightly
lighter so a logo and a button can sit on top. Dreamy, cheerful, cute. No text, no letters, no
characters, no logos, no watermark.
```

## Optional extras for later

These need more code work to fit in, so save them for a later round:

- **More rooms** (bedroom, hair salon, beach): reuse prompt 1 and change the room description. Keep
  "straight-on front view" and "floor in the bottom third, wide open and empty".
- **App icon:** "Cute app icon of a pastel rainbow egg wearing a small gold crown, glossy 3D-looking
  2D illustration, sparkles, soft pink and lavender background, centered, no text", square, 1024 × 1024.

### About painted dolls

To make the dolls themselves look hand-painted, every hairstyle and every piece of clothing would have to
be painted separately on a matching blank doll, with transparent backgrounds, all lined up exactly. Image
generators can't keep that many pieces consistent yet. So the dolls stay drawn by code for now, and
painted backgrounds give the biggest jump in quality.

# Interview Notes

## Part 1: The game she loves

**Favorite games:** Toca Boca World ("boca world") and other dress-up games.

**What you do:** Dress up, and "everything in the game" (explore, play with places and characters).

**Controls:** Tapping and swiping.

**Favorite parts:**
- Dressing up characters
- Buying things
- Unlocking things, and unlocking should be **easy**

**What she doesn't like:** (not answered yet)

## What this means for our game

- An open-ended dress-up game with no "losing", where you play however you want.
- Coins are earned in the game and never cost real money. No ads, no purchases.
- Unlocks come often and quickly, so she gets a new item almost every time she plays.
- Big buttons that work with a tap, and swiping to flip through clothes.

## Part 2: Designing our version

**Name:** Toca Toca Egg House

**Who we dress up:** Everyone, especially the family and friends. She asked for us to make the characters.

**What you can change:** "Pretty much everything": clothing, hair, hair color, shirts, dresses, shoes,
makeup, eyes, skin and nails.

**First place:** A castle.

**Ways to earn coins:**
- Finishing an outfit
- A daily gift box
- A mini game where you win a fashion show
- Some reward just for taking part

**Look and sound:** Bright rainbow and sparkly, with music.

**About the player:** 5 years old and just starting to read. The game should help her learn:
it should be able to read words out loud when she taps a button, but not read everything automatically.

**Parent note:** Photos or descriptions of the real family can come later. You can change everything about
each character in the game anyway.

# Toca Toca Egg House: Game Plan

*Read this one with her. Each line is something she asked for.*

## What is it?
A dress-up game in a rainbow castle. You dress up your family and friends however you like.
You can't lose. Just play!

## Who lives in the castle?
**Mommy, Daddy, Me and Sister.** Grown-ups can change their names. They walk around the castle,
wave, dance and talk to each other in speech bubbles. Tap a bubble to hear what they say! Everyone can be changed:
skin, eyes, eye color, hair, hair color, lipstick, face paint, tops, dresses, pants, skirts, shoes,
nails, hats and extras like wings, a cape or a magic wand.

## How do I get coins? 🪙
| Do this | You get |
|---|---|
| Finish an outfit and tap **✓ Done** | 10 coins |
| Open the **surprise egg** (once a day) | 25 coins + a free surprise item |
| Walk the runway in the **Fashion Show** | 17 to 32 coins. Everyone wins a prize! |

## How do I unlock things?
Lots of things are free. Things with a 🪙 price are locked. Tap one to **try it on**, then tap **✓ Buy**
if you like it. Prices are small (5 to 30 coins), so unlocking is easy.

## The Fashion Show 🌟
1. Find out today's theme: Royal Ball 👑, Rainbow Day 🌈, Pink Party 💖, Beach Day 🏖️, Snow Day ❄️,
   Fairy Garden 🧚 or Super Sparkly ✨.
2. Pick who walks the runway and dress them up for the theme.
3. Tap **Walk!** The judges, Bunny 🐰, Kitty 🐱 and Owl 🦉, each give up to 3 stars.
4. Win a medal or trophy, plus coins.

## Learning to read 📖
Words have a 🔊 button. Tap it, or tap the word, and the game reads it out loud. The word lights up
while it is read. Clothes are named with their color ("Pink Princess Gown"), so you learn color words
while you play. Grown-ups can turn on automatic reading in the grown-ups menu.

## Rainbow, sparkles and music
Bright rainbow colors, twinkling sparkles and a happy castle song. The 🎵 button turns the music off or on.

## For grown-ups
- Press and hold the ⚙️ gear for 1.5 seconds to open the grown-ups menu. You can rename people,
  turn the music and automatic reading on or off, give coins, or start over.
- No ads, no purchases, no accounts and no tracking. Everything is saved on the device.
